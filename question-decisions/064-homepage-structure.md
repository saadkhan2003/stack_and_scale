# Question 064 — Homepage structure

**Status:** Locked  
**Implementation:** Phase 1  
**Source:** Stack & Scale 100-question architecture interview

> The original conversational wording has been normalized for clarity. This record preserves the final decision after later refinements.

## Question

How should the homepage tell the company story?

## Your chosen direction

Use a professional product-led sequence: hero, work/products, proof, capabilities, delivery approach, trust and conversion.

## Why this was chosen

The structure explains value through evidence before asking the visitor to trust service claims.

## Why it is a strong fit

- Clear narrative
- Strong product emphasis
- Natural conversion path

## Tradeoffs and safeguards

Every section must earn its place; excessive content would weaken the story.

## Decision rule

This decision remains valid unless real implementation evidence, a legal requirement, or a material business-model change justifies revisiting it. Any revision should be recorded as a new architecture decision rather than silently changing this file.

Important point:

The homepage should **not start with services**.

Most software houses do:

> Web Development
> Mobile Apps
> AI Solutions
> Contact Us

That makes them look interchangeable.

Your homepage should first communicate:

> "We build real software products and reliable systems."

Then explain services.

#### Explanation or resolution

Locked: **Question 64 = homepage storytelling structure**, but yes — we should make it more professional and less like a normal agency landing page.

The previous structure is a good foundation, but for the company you want to become, I would refine it into a **software company/product-company style homepage**.

The homepage should feel closer to a serious technology company, while still being understandable for local businesses.

A stronger structure:

```text
1. Hero — Company Positioning

Clear statement:
"We build reliable software that businesses run on."

Supporting message:
Custom software, business platforms, AI automation, and digital products engineered for real-world operations.

Visual:
Interactive software ecosystem:
POS + Mobile App + Analytics + AI + Automation

CTAs:
[Explore Products]
[Book a Demo]


↓

2. Proof Immediately (Trust Layer)

Not empty claims.

Show:

✓ Industries supported

Client logos

3. Flagship Products

Retail stores, distributors, growing businesses

- Interface previews
```
