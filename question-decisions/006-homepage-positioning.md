# Question 006 — Homepage positioning

**Status:** Locked  
**Implementation:** Phase 1  
**Source:** Stack & Scale 100-question architecture interview

> The original conversational wording has been normalized for clarity. This record preserves the final decision after later refinements.

## Question

Should the homepage lead with products, services, both equally, or real software work?

## Your chosen direction

Lead with real software and products, then use that evidence to establish custom-engineering capability.

## Why this was chosen

Showing what the company has built is more persuasive than opening with a generic list of services.

## Why it is a strong fit

- Makes the company feel product-led
- Builds credibility quickly
- Differentiates Stack & Scale from ordinary agencies

## Tradeoffs and safeguards

The approach depends on high-quality product visuals and honest project evidence.

## Decision rule

This decision remains valid unless real implementation evidence, a legal requirement, or a material business-model change justifies revisiting it. Any revision should be recorded as a new architecture decision rather than silently changing this file.

### Discussion round 1

#### Question, options, and recommendation

### Question 6 — What should the homepage lead with?

This is one of the biggest decisions.

Should the homepage primarily present you as:

**A. A software product company**
Lead with POS, tailor management, business software, etc.

**B. A software development company**
Lead with custom software, Flutter, AI agents, automation, data analysis, etc.

**C. Both equally**
Products and services both visible immediately.

**D. A “we build real software” company**
Lead with the strongest projects/products first, then reveal that you also build custom systems for clients.

For your goals, I currently recommend **D**.

That would mean the homepage starts with something like:

> **We build software businesses depend on.**

Then immediately show real work:
- POS platform
- Tailor management system
- Mobile app
- AI automation
- Dashboard/data product

And only after the visitor has seen proof do we explain:

> Need something custom? We design and build software for your business.

This is much stronger than opening with six service cards.

**Would you choose D, or do you want A/B/C instead?**

#### Your response

> D

#### Explanation or resolution

Perfect — **D is locked**.

So the homepage will not behave like a normal agency site. It will lead with **real software you have built**, then use that work to prove that your company can also build serious custom systems.

That gives us a stronger positioning:

> **We build software businesses depend on.**

Then the homepage can flow roughly like:

**Hero → Featured work/products → Proof/results → What we build → How we work → Client trust → CTA**
