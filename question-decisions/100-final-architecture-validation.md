# Question 100 — Final architecture validation

**Status:** Locked  
**Implementation:** Approved target architecture  
**Source:** Stack & Scale 100-question architecture interview

> The original conversational wording has been normalized for clarity. This record preserves the final decision after later refinements.

## Question

Does the complete architecture form a coherent production-ready direction?

## Your chosen direction

Approve a CMS-driven public platform, API-first modular backend, PostgreSQL core, standards-based identity, separated applications, Hetzner infrastructure and disciplined phased delivery.

## Why this was chosen

The final architecture supports products and services, local and international customers, strong operations, security and future expansion without making AI or enterprise-scale complexity mandatory now.

## Why it is a strong fit

- Provides one authoritative target
- Aligns product, sales and engineering
- Creates a stable basis for implementation planning

## Tradeoffs and safeguards

Implementation still requires provider selection, requirements, content, budgets and acceptance criteria; approval does not remove delivery discipline.

## Decision rule

This decision remains valid unless real implementation evidence, a legal requirement, or a material business-model change justifies revisiting it. Any revision should be recorded as a new architecture decision rather than silently changing this file.
