# Phase 14.1 Evidence: Shared Commercial Primitives

## Scope

Implemented provider-neutral contracts in `packages/contracts` for integer
minor-unit money, ISO-shaped currency codes, exact decimal rounding, configured
tax and discount calculations, document numbering, immutable issued versions,
and explicit commercial status transitions.

Money is signed so credits and compensating corrections can be represented. The
commercial calculation boundary rejects negative prices, quantities, rates and
discounts. Tax codes, rates and jurisdictions are input configuration; no
jurisdictional truth, payment provider or e-sign provider is enabled.

## Verification

Focused tests cover currency mismatch, safe integer and rounding boundaries,
zero and negative handling, configured tax and discount calculation, numbering
uniqueness, deep immutability of issued versions, and invalid status transitions.

## Ownership and follow-up

`packages/contracts` owns these shared contracts and tests. Later proposal,
contract, invoice and accounting work must preserve issued-version immutability
and use compensating records for corrections. Tax and numbering policy remain
blocked on the external legal, tax and finance decision gates in the Phase 14
execution plan.
