# Content and Evidence Inventory

## Rule

Only approved, truthful material may be published. Missing material is a tracked gap, not an invitation to invent proof.

| Content type | Current status | Required before public publishing | Owner | V1 treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logo and approved colors | Approved direction per Q061; source files not yet supplied to repository | Exact source path or source-of-record link, rights/ownership confirmation, usage rules and accessibility review owner | Brand owner | Do not embed until source material is supplied and recorded |
| Typography and extended identity | Not finalized | Design-system decision and licenses | Phase 02 | Define before public design implementation |
| Product records (POS, tailor management, others) | Needs inventory | Name, audience, features, screenshots, support/contact path, approval | Product owner | Publish only verified records |
| Custom services | Needs inventory | Scope, outcomes, capabilities, exclusions and CTA | Business owner | Build capability-first pages |
| Work/case studies | Needs approval inventory | Client permission, problem, solution, proof, privacy classification | Business/content owner | Target 3–5 truthful flagship experiences; defer count if assets are unavailable |
| Testimonials/client logos | Unknown | Written permission, exact wording/logo rights, source | Business owner | Omit until approved |
| Team/about/careers | Needs inventory | Current facts, portraits/bios, job details and approval | Company owner | Omit incomplete claims |
| Resources/guides | Not yet authored | Author, accuracy review, publication date, related CTA | Content owner | Seed only verified foundational content |
| Legal/privacy/cookie notices | Not drafted | Actual data practices, processor list, retention and contact route | Company owner + legal review | Required for production, never placeholder |

## Evidence classification

- **Publishable:** approved and factually supported.
- **Private:** can inform internal planning but may not appear publicly.
- **Incomplete:** missing required evidence; cannot publish.
- **Demonstration-only:** clearly labelled prototype/example, never represented as a customer result.

## Intake checklist

For every item record: owner, source files, rights/permission, factual claims, personal-data classification, approval date, expiry/review date, related CMS ID and public route. Store no unnecessary client data in this inventory.
