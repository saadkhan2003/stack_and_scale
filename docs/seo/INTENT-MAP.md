# Initial public-search intent map

This is a content-planning map, not a keyword-stuffing or location-page plan.
Each topic must only be published when its associated offering and evidence are
approved in the CMS inventory.

| Audience | Intent | First useful content | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pakistani product buyers | Improve retail/service operations with dependable local support | Product and industry pages focused on operational clarity | Book a demo or discuss a project |
| Pakistani custom-software buyers | Evaluate a delivery partner for a constrained business workflow | Service, approach and illustrative-work pages | Discuss your project |
| International engineering buyers | Find a focused product/design/engineering delivery partner | Services, approach and technical resource pages | Discuss your project |
| International product teams | Validate capability before a distributed engagement | Product, resource and approved case-study pages | Start a conversation |

No city landing pages, translated duplicates, invented customer metrics or
unverified local-business schema are permitted. Country, office, language and
contact claims are added only after the business owner supplies accurate,
approved information.
