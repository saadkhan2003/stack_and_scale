# Step 13.7 - Notifications and Inbox

## Contract

Notifications are durable rows scoped by organization and recipient. Each row
has a category, urgency, bounded same-workspace deep link, read state, dedupe
key and email delivery state. Repeated `(organization, recipient, dedupe key)`
events return the original row.

## Authorization and safety

All staff roles may read their own notifications and manage their own category
preferences. The API resolves organization and actor from the existing tenant
membership policy; recipient input is only accepted when it is an active member
of that resolved organization. Security notices and critical urgency always
queue email and cannot be disabled. Deep links are limited to `/staff/` paths.

## Delivery

Critical or enabled notification email is represented by the existing
`notification.email` outbox event. The worker sends through the existing
transactional email adapter and updates notification delivery state; retries
remain governed by the existing outbox policy.

## Verification

Focused coverage is in `apps/api/test/notifications.integration.test.ts` and
`apps/web/test/staff-notifications.test.ts`. Evidence must not include tokens,
secrets or personal notification content.
