# Step 13.8 - Knowledge and Operating Procedures

Knowledge is stored in `platform.knowledge_articles`, keyed by organization.
Articles have one accountable owner, a required review timestamp, bounded body
text, one of procedure/script/FAQ/onboarding types, and draft/published/archived
status. Read access is available to every active staff member; create, update,
and delete require `knowledge:manage` (owner, admin, or manager). Every
mutation is audit recorded. Published and non-published content is never
returned outside the authenticated staff API. The title and body are mirrored
into the existing tenant-filtered operations search index; delete removes the
mirror.
