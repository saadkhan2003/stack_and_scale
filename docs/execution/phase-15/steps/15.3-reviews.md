# 15.3 Reviews And Approvals

## Outcome

Allow assigned client reviewers to make audited decisions against the exact
proposal or deliverable version they received.

## Inputs and dependencies

- Source decisions: 46, 51, 56, 59, 65, 70, 84, 88-89.
- Predecessors: `15.0-contract-freeze`, `15.1-access`, `15.2-home-projects`.
- Inputs: version-bound approval rules and canonical proposal/file projections.

## Ownership and compatibility

- Owns: portal review request/decision adapters, DTOs, routes and tests.
- Prohibited: canonical proposal, contract and deliverable version mutation
  semantics.
- Output: idempotent client decisions with actor, timestamp, comment and exact
  version evidence.
- Impact: additive portal events; canonical approval rules remain authoritative.

## Actions and verification

1. Reject expired, revoked or stale review targets.
2. Bind each decision to the rendered version/checksum and audit authorization.
3. Surface client-safe revision state without internal review commentary.
4. Test replay, stale versions, cross-customer access and reviewer removal.
- Evidence: `docs/evidence/phase-15/15.3-reviews/`.
- Security: enforce fresh session where the frozen contract requires it.
- Capacity/cost: use idempotency keys and bounded decision-history queries.
- Rollback: disable `portal-approvals`; canonical staff approval remains usable.
- Merge order: after `15.2`; frozen contract version required.
