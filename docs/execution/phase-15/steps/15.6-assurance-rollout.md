# 15.6 Assurance, Capacity And Rollout

## Outcome

Establish evidence that the portal is isolated, accessible, observable and safe
for staged organization-level rollout.

## Inputs and dependencies

- Source decisions: 15, 17, 28-43, 46, 51, 56, 59, 65, 70, 79, 84, 88-89.
- Predecessors: `15.1-access` through `15.5-support-activity`.
- Inputs: all portal flags, DTOs, test suites, capacity ledger and rollback plan.

## Ownership and compatibility

- Owns: Phase 15 verification/evidence, browser scenarios, capacity updates and
  rollout controls.
- Prohibited: canonical data deletion, permanent broad enablement and unreviewed
  shared-contract changes.
- Output: tenant-isolation/security reports, accessibility/mobile evidence,
  measured capacity headroom and staged rollout record.
- Impact: operational configuration/evidence only.

## Actions and verification

1. Run API, integration, browser and accessibility checks for every portal flag.
2. Prove cross-organization/customer denials and DTO forbidden-field absence.
3. Test session/member revocation, signed URL expiry, stale decisions and
   independent feature-flag rollback.
4. Measure authenticated sessions, project queries, transfer, outbox and support
   load; update `docs/operations/CAPACITY-LEDGER.md` with scale trigger/cost.
- Evidence: `docs/evidence/phase-15/15.6-assurance-rollout/`.
- Security: block release on any internal/public separation failure.
- Capacity/cost: establish safe headroom and a priced next topology before rollout.
- Rollback: revoke portal sessions, stop URLs and disable organization flags.
- Merge order: last; references the frozen contract version and all prior steps.
