# 15.2 Home And Projects

## Outcome

Expose a calm client home and project views using allow-listed projections of
approved scope, milestones, deliverables, actions and client-safe activity.

## Inputs and dependencies

- Source decisions: 28-43, 51, 70, 79, 84.
- Predecessors: `15.0-contract-freeze`, `15.1-access`.
- Inputs: frozen visibility matrix, access principal and canonical read APIs.

## Ownership and compatibility

- Owns: portal home/project DTOs, projection queries, portal web routes and
  tests.
- Prohibited: canonical project/customer records and staff views.
- Output: bounded, paginated home/project projections and client-safe status
  history.
- Impact: additive portal API/read models; no `SELECT *` or staff serialization.

## Actions and verification

1. Implement DTO allow-lists for every response and event.
2. Scope every project by organization plus explicit customer/project grant.
3. Render pending actions and milestones without staff assignments, estimates or
   internal risks.
4. Test empty, revoked, cross-customer, mobile and low-bandwidth states.
- Evidence: `docs/evidence/phase-15/15.2-home-projects/`.
- Security: assert forbidden fields are absent from API, search and activity.
- Capacity/cost: add pagination/query caps and measure dashboard load.
- Rollback: disable `portal-home` or `portal-projects` independently.
- Merge order: after `15.1`; consume the frozen contract version.
