# 15.5 Support, Activity And Notifications

## Outcome

Provide public-only customer support, client-safe activity and notification
preferences while making internal content impossible to retrieve or deliver.

## Inputs and dependencies

- Source decisions: 51, 56, 59, 65, 70, 79, 84, 88-89.
- Predecessors: `15.0-contract-freeze`, `15.1-access`, `15.2-home-projects`.
- Inputs: Phase 14 ticket/comment/file/communication records and event allow-list.

## Ownership and compatibility

- Owns: portal support/activity/notification DTOs, projections, routes and tests.
- Prohibited: canonical support records, internal comments, escalation state,
  staff notification preferences and delivery-audit internals.
- Output: client ticket/comment workflow, approved event feed and preferences.
- Impact: additive portal event projections only.

## Actions and verification

1. Filter comments, attachments, searches, exports and notifications to public
   allow-listed content before serialization.
2. Enforce customer ownership for creates and public replies.
3. Make activity explicit-event based and preserve mandatory security notices.
4. Test internal-note/attachment non-disclosure across all projection paths.
- Evidence: `docs/evidence/phase-15/15.5-support-activity/`.
- Security: test notification recipient and event-metadata isolation.
- Capacity/cost: batch/paginate feeds; measure outbox and support query load.
- Rollback: disable support/activity/notification flags independently.
- Merge order: after `15.2`; frozen contract version required.
