# 16.4 — licenses and installations

## Outcome

Register license grants and installation identities with revocation, lease and
offline-grace behavior plus versioned signing-key metadata.

## Inputs and ownership

- Sources: decisions 028, 031, 033, 040, 051, 079, 084, 085 and frozen
  `16.0–16.3`.
- Owns: license/installation schema, lease API, key-metadata contract and
  recovery workflows.
- Prohibits: private signing keys, product runtime databases and mass-revocation
  automation.

## Actions and compatibility

1. Bind installations to explicit licenses and product-account organizations.
2. Prevent lease rollback and stale entitlement replay.
3. Define replacement, reactivation, key rotation overlap and compromised-key
   recovery with staff audit.

## Verification and evidence

- Test revocation, replay, offline grace, key rotation and compromised-key
  simulations.
- Evidence: `docs/evidence/phase-16/16.4-licenses-installations/`.
- Capacity/cost: bounded lease checks and registry indexes; $0 change.
- Rollback: suspend new activations, preserve legitimate existing leases.
- Merge: after `16.3`; consumes the frozen snapshot version.
