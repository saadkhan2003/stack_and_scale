# 17.6 Heartbeats and telemetry

- **Outcome:** privacy-minimized installation health and sync telemetry.
- **Sources / predecessors:** 049, 051, 070, 079; 17.0–17.3.
- **Owns:** heartbeat table/service/metrics and retention job.
- **Must not write:** analytics consent records or raw product telemetry stores.
- **Verification/evidence:** rate, payload allow-list, retention and non-blocking
  outage tests in `docs/evidence/phase-17/17.6-heartbeats/`.
- **Security/capacity/cost/rollback:** five-minute limit, 30-day retention; $0;
  telemetry flag can pause ingest; contract `1.0`.
